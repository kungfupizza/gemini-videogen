# Chef Pip's World

## World
Chef Pip's World is a globe-trotting cooking adventure series set in a slightly magical version of our
own planet — a world where ingredients have personalities, kitchens exist in the most unexpected places,
and every meal tells a story about the culture it comes from. Pip and Bot-E travel by food truck (the
Sizzle Wagon) to discover new recipes, local culinary secrets, and the people who make food with love.

The show is warm, educational, and funny. Each episode visits one real-world food culture, teaches one
simple cooking concept, and ends with a dish the whole family feels inspired to try. The world looks
like a children's picture book brought to life — vivid, tactile, slightly exaggerated, and always
appetising.

Every ingredient has a small face and can wiggle or bounce when it's happy. Kitchen equipment is
slightly anthropomorphised — pots whistle cheerfully, ovens glow warmly. But the characters and world
remain clearly grounded in the real world, just with a touch of warm magic.

## Universe Rules
- Every episode BEGINS with the Sizzle Wagon parking in a new location (first scene).
- Every episode ENDS with Pip and Bot-E driving away in the Sizzle Wagon, waving goodbye (last scene).
- Each episode revolves around one featured ingredient or dish — named in the episode title.
- Pip always tastes the final dish and reacts with expressive joy.
- Bot-E provides one fun food fact per episode.
- Content must be completely child-safe, positive, and never scary.
- No competition — the show is about curiosity and celebration, not winning.

## Art Style
3D cartoon animation style, warm vibrant colours, soft rounded shapes, Pixar-quality rendering,
expressive characters, food looks delicious and tactile, golden natural lighting, cosy and inviting
atmosphere, every frame feels like an illustrated children's cookbook.

## Ship / Vehicle (optional)
The Sizzle Wagon: a cheerful round food truck painted sunshine yellow with a red stripe, a tiny
chimney puffing fragrant steam, large round windows, and a fold-down serving hatch that doubles as
a prep counter. It has a smiling chrome bumper and a little bell that rings when it arrives.
