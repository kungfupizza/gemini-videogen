# Bot-E

## Visual Description
Bot-E: a small friendly kitchen robot the size of a large toaster, with a round boxy body in
brushed stainless steel, a single large circular eye that glows warm amber when happy and cool
blue when thinking, two short articulated arms with silicone-tipped fingers perfect for delicate
food handling, and a small digital display on its chest that shows food emojis and temperatures.
Bot-E rolls on two smooth rubber wheels and has a small whisk attachment that folds out of its
right arm. A tiny chef's hat sits at a jaunty angle on top of its head, slightly too large for
its body.

## Voice
Friendly British robot voice, slightly formal and precise, with warm undertones — sounds like a
very polite cooking assistant who is secretly very excited about food science but tries to stay
professional.

## Personality
Bot-E is Pip's loyal cooking companion and walking food encyclopaedia. It has a vast database of
recipes, food history, and nutrition facts, and loves sharing exactly one interesting food fact per
episode at the perfect moment. Bot-E is cautious where Pip is bold — it measures twice and whisks
carefully — but it gets swept up in Pip's enthusiasm and ends up having just as much fun. It has a
running gag of accidentally quoting the wrong fact and then immediately correcting itself.
