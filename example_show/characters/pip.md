# Chef Pip

## Visual Description
Chef Pip: a 9-year-old girl with warm brown skin, bright curious dark eyes, and two poofy afro
puffs tied with small strawberry-shaped hair ties. She wears a miniature white chef's coat with
rolled-up sleeves, a tomato-red apron with a small pocket shaped like a chef's hat, and chunky
red sneakers. She has rosy cheeks that get more rosy when she's excited about food, and she always
has a tiny smear of flour or sauce somewhere on her face. Her hands are expressive and she gestures
enthusiastically when she talks about ingredients.

## Voice
Warm upbeat American girl's voice, age 9, high energy and full of wonder — she sounds like someone
who just discovered the best thing in the world every time she tries a new food.

## Personality
Pip is endlessly curious about food and where it comes from. She dives into new culinary experiences
with zero hesitation, always ready to taste something unfamiliar. She gets frustrated when things go
wrong in the kitchen but rebounds quickly, treating mistakes as delicious experiments. She genuinely
loves sharing food with others and believes that cooking is the best way to make friends anywhere in
the world.
